Your privacy, in plain language

Privacy Policy

Boston Baseball Hub, shown as Hub Ball on your device, is designed to help fans follow Boston baseball without creating an account or being tracked.

Effective September 2, 2026

The short version

  • No account is required.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not include third-party analytics.

Information we collect

We do not ask for or intentionally collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, health information, financial information, or advertising identifier. The app does not provide user accounts, personalized advertising, or user-generated public profiles.

Live data and network requests

The app uses an internet connection to retrieve scores, schedules, standings, player statistics, news summaries, public social posts, and related images. These requests are made to Boston Baseball Hub services, the MLB Stats API, and—in some cases—the publishers or platforms that host linked images and content.

Like ordinary website traffic, those services may receive standard technical information needed to deliver a response, such as an IP address, request time, and basic network headers. We do not use that information to build advertising profiles or track you across apps and websites.

External articles and social posts

Headlines and social posts may open websites operated by MLB, newspapers, X, or other third parties. Their websites and apps are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. Boston Baseball Hub does not control how those third parties handle information after you leave the app.

Information stored on your device

iOS may temporarily cache app content to improve speed and reliability. Any system-managed cache remains under the control of your device and can be removed by deleting the app. We do not use this cache to identify you.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because it has no accounts, advertising, or user-submitted content, it does not ask children to provide personal information.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated before the new practices take effect. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Questions and feedback

During the beta, use Send Beta Feedback on the app's TestFlight page. TestFlight will route your message and optional screenshot directly to the developer.

Boston Baseball Hub is an independent, unofficial fan app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Major League Baseball, the Boston Red Sox, or any publisher or social platform referenced in the app.
